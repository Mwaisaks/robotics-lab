# Robot Arm Lab: roadmap (future work, NOT built yet)

Source: Joshua's longer vision (robotics trainer, Tukuze Afrika). Everything below is
**planned, not built**. Don't describe any of it as a current feature.

## Built today
- Level 1, "Sort the parts": hands-only sorting on your real table, with a robot arm that shows its reach.

## Next
- **More sorting activities:** sort by size or weight, sort by shape only, timed and relaxed modes.
- **Level 3: electronics bench.** Build a simple LED circuit on a virtual breadboard: battery, resistor, LED, wires. Then connect a generic "microcontroller board" and make the LED blink. In the app the board stays generic, with no brand names or logos.
- **Robot construction:** assemble a small robot from parts (chassis, wheels, motors, sensor), then test it.
- **Level 4: advanced lab:**
  - joint control: move each joint of the arm by hand and see its angle
  - 2-link inverse kinematics: drag the gripper target and watch the joints solve
  - PID control: tune a simple controller and watch it settle

## Principles for every level
- Hands-first, seated, readable in a narrow field of view.
- Icons and at most 5 words per instruction, for secondary school students and young learners.
- Short sessions (under 10 minutes), and it must work in rooms we never tested.
